Chrome History & Data Safety Guide

How to safely clear browser history without losing data

Why Does Data Disappear When History Is Cleared?

When using Chrome's "Clear browsing data", the option "Cookies and other site data" is selected by default. This option wipes the offline storage of all web apps installed on your device.

Recommended 3-Step Cleaning Method:

  1. Open Chrome and navigate to History → Clear browsing data.
  2. Keep "Browsing history" and "Cached images and files" checked.
  3. UNCHECK "Cookies and other site data"!

Your browser history will be completely cleaned, while your NetPay Pro application data remains 100% safe and intact.

Recovery Options if Data is Ever Missing:

  • 1. Restore from Cloud: Click "Restore Cloud" under Settings to fetch the latest automatic cloud copy.
  • 2. Download Backup File: Keep periodic .json backup files in your Downloads folder. Files in your device's Downloads are never wiped by Chrome.
  • 3. Storage Protection Shield: NetPay Pro utilizes IndexedDB dual storage and automated daily snapshots for enhanced data safety.
